Dealing statistics

Each trial shuffles a full deck, deals 8 cards to each player, then deals alternately until the deck is empty — so you finish with 30 of the 60 cards. Each trial then measures, per colour, how many cards you got and what they are worth. Both are reported by rank: the five values are sorted, so group 1 is whichever colour came out worst that trial and group 5 whichever came out best. The groups are ranks, not colours — group 1 is red in one trial, blue in the next.
