How to play

Build ventures: ascending runs of one colour in your play area. Wagers (×2) must come before any numbers and multiply that venture.

A venture scores (sum of numbers − 20) × (1 + wagers), plus +20 if it reaches 8 cards. The −20 is the cost of starting — short ventures lose points.

Your turn: ① play one card to a venture, or discard it · ② draw from the deck or a discard-pile top (not the pile you just discarded to).

The game ends when the draw pile empties. Highest total wins.
